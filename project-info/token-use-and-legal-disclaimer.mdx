## ⚠️ Token Use & Risk Disclosure

### 🎯 Intended Use of $PAYAI:

- Buy PayAI facilitator credits at a 10% discount by paying with $PAYAI on Solana, either in the [merchant portal](https://merchant.payai.network) or when an agent [buys an API key and credits over x402](/x402/facilitators/agent-api-keys). See [Facilitator Pricing](/x402/facilitators/pricing).
- $PAYAI paid for credits is burned. PayAI burns it on Solana after the purchase, which permanently removes it from the token supply. The burn transactions are public on-chain.

$PAYAI is **not required** to use the PayAI platform. Credits can also be bought with USDC at the standard price.
