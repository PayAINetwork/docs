## Solana batch settlement

[Batch settlement](/x402/servers/batch-settlement) is available as a public preview for USDC. The public `/supported` endpoint advertises its dedicated fee payer and admission limits. Its `/verify` and `/settle` requests require a merchant API key, even within the free tier.
